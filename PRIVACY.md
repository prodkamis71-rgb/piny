# Sprintera Studio — Privacy Policy

Sprintera Studio is a private desktop tool used by one person, the owner of the Sprintera shop on Etsy,
to prepare and publish posts about their own products on their own Pinterest, Instagram and TikTok
accounts.

- It signs in only through each platform's official OAuth or token flow; it never asks for or stores
  passwords.
- Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are used only for the owner's account.
- It reads only the owner's own boards, posts and their statistics. It does not collect, sell or share
  data about any other person.
- Disconnecting in the tool's settings deletes the stored token; access can also be revoked in each
  platform's account settings.

Contact: through Etsy Messages to the Sprintera shop.
